Chat vs. board

Everything else is a chat window.

Same agent, same work. But a transcript hands it to you one line at a time, so knowing where you stand means scrolling back and rebuilding it in your head. A board just shows you. What's running, what's blocked and what's waiting on you become places you can point at.
